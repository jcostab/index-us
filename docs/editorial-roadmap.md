# Index Us editorial roadmap

This is the recommended ten-article launch queue. Treat each item as a brief, not a pre-approved claim. Recheck current primary sources before drafting, record the research date, and follow `EDITORIAL.md`.

| Priority | Working title | Reader value | Format | Freshness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The week in AI: what actually changed? | A concise weekly digest separating shipped changes from demos and rumours | News roundup | Weekly |
| 2 | The AI model chooser: match the model to the job | A decision framework based on privacy, latency, tool use, context and cost | Evergreen guide | Quarterly |
| 3 | Local, private or cloud? Where your AI workload should run | Helps teams choose an architecture before choosing a vendor | Analysis | Six-monthly |
| 4 | MCP explained without the protocol soup | Shows readers what connected tools enable, where permissions sit and what can go wrong | Explainer | Quarterly |
| 5 | Build your first useful AI evaluation in an afternoon | A downloadable, small-team test method using real tasks and failure scoring | Tutorial | Quarterly |
| 6 | New AI tool review: evidence before enthusiasm | A repeatable hands-on review of one newly released tool, including access, price and limitations | Tool review | At release |
| 7 | Prompting after agents: five techniques that still matter | Separates durable instruction design from obsolete prompt folklore | Practical guide | Quarterly |
| 8 | AI data privacy checklist for small organisations | Questions to ask before staff upload customer or internal data | Checklist | Quarterly |
| 9 | What an AI benchmark can—and cannot—tell you | Teaches readers to inspect baselines, contamination, task fit and independent replication | Explainer | Six-monthly |
| 10 | From prototype to dependable agent: the missing production layer | Covers permissions, observability, evals, fallbacks, budgets and human escalation | Field guide | Quarterly |

## Recommended launch mix

Publish one timely weekly digest, two explainers, two practical guides and one tool review first. This demonstrates freshness without making the publication dependent on a daily news treadmill.
